Chapter 02

Our Story
Dr. Lotilla Lara
Est. 2006

Before the clinic,
there was a question.

Lotilla Lara grew up watching her grandmother squint at the world — at family photographs, at menu boards, at the faces of grandchildren she could not quite make out. No one had ever told her she needed glasses.

That image stayed with Lotilla through school, through her optometry training, through the early years of practice in small shared spaces. It is why she opened her own clinic in 2006: not to run a business, but to ensure that no one in her community would go through life seeing less than they should.

Eighteen years later, that purpose has not changed. Every consultation at Lotilla Lara Optical begins the same way — not with a chart, but with a conversation.

"I am not fitting lenses. I am giving someone their world back." — Dr. Lotilla Lara, Founder

Chapter 06

Your Journey

From first visit
to clear vision.

Every patient follows the same careful path. Here is what to expect.

Step 01

Book Your Appointment

Choose a time that suits you — online, by phone, or via WhatsApp. New patients receive a pre-visit health questionnaire by email.

Step 02

The Consultation

A conversation first. We ask about your vision, your lifestyle, and your daily environment. Only then does the examination begin.

Step 03

Full Eye Examination

Visual acuity, intraocular pressure, retinal health, and — where indicated — digital retinal photography. Thorough, unhurried, precise.

Step 04

Prescription & Guidance

Your results, explained in plain language. Dr. Lara walks you through every finding and answers every question before moving forward.

Step 05

Frame Selection

Guided styling session. We match frames to your face shape, colouring, and lifestyle — not just your prescription. Take your time. This part matters.

Step 06

Fitting & Collection

Lenses precision-cut and fitted in-house. Collection appointment includes a final comfort and vision check. Adjustments made on the spot.

Ongoing

Annual Review & Aftercare

Free frame adjustments for life. Annual review reminders sent automatically. You are a patient here, not a transaction.
