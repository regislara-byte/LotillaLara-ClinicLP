# [0.12.0] — Operations Dashboard

Status

🚧 Active Development

Date

2026-06

---

## Objective

Begin development of the private Operations Dashboard that connects to the existing production backend.

The public website remains customer-facing.

The dashboard becomes the operational workspace for clinic staff.

---

## Engineering Decision

Implementation_012 builds upon the completed production backend.

The backend architecture remains unchanged.

No redesign.

No duplicate database.

No direct Google Sheets communication from JavaScript.

Google Apps Script remains the single backend interface.
