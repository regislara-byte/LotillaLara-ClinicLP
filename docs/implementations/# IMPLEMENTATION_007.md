## Project

## Title

EYLOTL™ Motion System

## Framework

Applied Engineering AI + Human Intelligence

## Status

READY FOR BUILD ✅

---

## Objective

Introduce subtle motion and premium interactions to EYLOTL™ without affecting:

* Performance
* Accessibility
* Readability
* Trust
* Mobile experience

The clinic remains the primary message.

EYLOTL™ remains a supporting brand mascot.

---

## Design Philosophy

Motion should be felt, not noticed.

Visitors should perceive:

* Life
* Warmth
* Personality
* Modern polish

Visitors should NOT perceive:

* Cartoon behavior
* Gaming effects
* Excessive movement
* Visual distraction

---

## Motion Layer 001

### Idle Bob

Purpose:

Add subtle life to EYLOTL™.

Behavior:

* Gentle vertical movement
* Maximum ±6px
* Slow easing
* Infinite loop

Impact:

Low

---

## Motion Layer 002

### Eye Blink

Purpose:

Create natural presence.

Behavior:

* Blink every 4–6 seconds
* Non-distracting
* Realistic timing
* No exaggerated animation

Impact:

Medium

---

## Motion Layer 003

### Hover Tilt

Purpose:

Increase engagement.

Behavior:

* Small mouse-follow tilt
* Maximum 3° rotation
* Desktop only
* Smooth easing

Impact:

Medium

---

## Motion Layer 004

### Scroll Parallax

Purpose:

Add depth to the hero experience.

Behavior:

* Subtle movement relative to scroll
* Preserve readability
* Maintain performance

Impact:

Low

---

## Motion Safety Rules

EYLOTL™ must remain a trusted healthcare mascot.

Avoid:

* Fast movement
* Sudden flashing
* Cartoon exaggeration
* Aggressive scaling
* Gameplay interactions

Required Feel:

* Calm
* Professional
* Human
* Premium
* Friendly

---

## Accessibility

All motion must respect:

prefers-reduced-motion

If enabled:

* Disable animation
* Disable hover tilt
* Disable parallax
* Preserve full functionality

---

## Performance Requirements

Rules:

* CSS-first animations
* Minimal JavaScript
* No animation libraries
* Mobile-safe
* Maintain Lighthouse performance
* Maintain responsive behavior

---

## Files Allowed To Change

Primary:

* style.css

Secondary:

* script.js (only if required)

Avoid changing:

* Content
* Navigation
* Booking flow
* Location section
* Daily Report section
* Journey section

---

Visitors should NOT feel:
